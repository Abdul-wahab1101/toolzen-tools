Convert a Word DOCX File to PDF

Convert DOCX documents to PDF in your browser. Basic text, headings and common tables are supported; complex layouts may change.

This browser version converts DOCX text and common headings/tables to PDF. Some complex layouts or images may change. Older .doc files are not supported.

How to use

  1. Choose a .docx file under 15 MB.
  2. Select Convert Word to PDF.
  3. Review the rendered content and page breaks.
  4. Download or share the resulting PDF.

Frequently asked questions

Which Word format is supported?

This version accepts .docx files. Older .doc files are not supported.

Will formatting be exactly identical?

Basic text, headings and common tables are converted. Complex layouts, fonts and floating images may change.

Are Word files uploaded?

No. The DOCX is read and converted in your browser.

Can I convert a large document?

For browser performance, this version limits DOCX files to 15 MB. Very long documents may take longer.
