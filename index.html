Privacy comes first

For this starter version, image processing runs on your device using your browser. Files are not intentionally sent to a server.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. This page uses your browser's image decoding and canvas features. The selected image is processed locally; there is no image-upload endpoint in this starter version.

Will every image reach exactly 20 KB?

Not always. The tool tries to stay at or below your chosen maximum by adjusting quality and dimensions. Extremely small targets may not be achievable while keeping useful image quality. The actual output size is always shown.

Which formats are supported?

This starter supports common JPG, PNG and WebP images. Some formats such as HEIC may not open in every browser.
